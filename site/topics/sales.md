## Pre-Call Research
tactic, in 2 videos.

Alex says research before a call is worth doing, with about 10% of the call time spent in prep for work you do all the time, and that a few minutes of research builds rapport quickly.

> "my rule of thumb for research is that if it's something that you do all the time it's about 10% of the time that you're going to be on a call or meeting" ([Sales Was Hard Until I Understood These 9 Concepts @ 12:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cy2k1GdA-9o&t=762s))

## Assumed Close
tactic, in 2 videos.

Asking a question that assumes the customer does not want more, such as 'you don't want anything else, do you?', so that the customer says no and thereby agrees to the add-on. Alex says it can produce 90 percent plus take rates.

> "And by doing that, you automatically close the sale that you just recommended." ([How To Close Prospects By Saying "No" First (ALEX HORMOZI) @ 4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j1tA4l7R2c0&t=278s))

> "you can literally see 90% plus take rates on this, right?" ([How To Close Prospects By Saying "No" First (ALEX HORMOZI) @ 5:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j1tA4l7R2c0&t=340s))

> "And the key was I didn't ask her whether she wanted them. I asked her which one she wanted." ([The "Assumed Close": How to Close Deals Without Resistance @ 3:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xy1h4qqF778&t=185s))
