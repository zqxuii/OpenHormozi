# Finite Versus Infinite Game

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi, citing Simon Sinek, says most worthwhile pursuits are infinite games where the aim is to keep playing, so applying finite rules to them causes failure.

## His words

> "Finite games where you have known known players, agreed upon rules, um and an outcome that that wins the game"
> [Success Is Just a Side Effect of Following These Principles @ 63:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JuNOFW-oVn8&t=3819s)

> "the point of the game is to keep playing. That's the point."
> [Success Is Just a Side Effect of Following These Principles @ 65:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JuNOFW-oVn8&t=3942s)

> "whenever you have a infinite game player who's going against a finite player, the infinite player always wins"
