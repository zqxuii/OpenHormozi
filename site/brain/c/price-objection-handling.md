# Price Objection Handling

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to keep the frame after a price objection by agreeing with the prospect and asking a follow-up question; most sales should close at the ask, not through objection overcoming.

## His words

> "you have to agree with what they're saying and then simply ask a follow-up question"

> "you will close the majority of your sales after you ask them to buy if they are qualified to buy"
