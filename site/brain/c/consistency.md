# Consistency

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: principle | Seen in 6 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi, after Charlie Munger, says consistency is one of the hardest virtues because people are drawn to novelty, and that not being consistent guarantees you will not reach success.

## His words

> "consistency doesn't guarantee that you'll be successful but not being consistent will guarantee that you won't reach success"
