# Ignorance Debt

Topic: Sales | Type: principle | Seen in 17 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex retells a sales speaker's whiteboard story: a $50,000 earner pays $950,000 a year for not knowing how to make a million, so the value of a skill is that gap.
