# Own Your Circumstances

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: mindset | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says people who give power to circumstances or other people cast their power away, so the only useful response is to own what you can control.

## His words

> "Do not cast power to your excuses. Own your circumstances because no one else will."
