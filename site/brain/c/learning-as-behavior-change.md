# Learning as Behavior Change

Topic: Wealth & Investing | Type: principle | Seen in 8 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi defines learning as same condition, new behaviour, so if you meet the same situation and do not change how you act, you have not learned.

## His words

> "learning simply means same condition new Behavior so the same thing happens and then you respond differently"
> [How to Win (At ANYTHING) @ 16:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MNll1BaskLA&t=986s)

> "if you do not change you did not learn"
> [How to Win (At ANYTHING) @ 46:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MNll1BaskLA&t=2811s)

> "learning is same condition new Behavior so to the point I felt sad last time I learned this new thing"
