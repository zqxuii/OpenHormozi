# Core Four

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: framework | Seen in 41 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says that when starting out you should spend four hours a day on the four ways one person can reach people: one-to-one outreach to people you know, one-to-one outreach to strangers, one-to-many content, and one-to-many ads.
