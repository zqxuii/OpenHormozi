# Sales Process

Topic: Sales | Type: framework | Seen in 92 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex describes learning sales by watching others ask why someone is there, ask about their goals and ask about past attempts that did not work, and then asking more questions rather than accepting a no.

## His words

> "they start with asking them why someone's there ask them about their goals ask them things that they've done in the past that didn't work"

> "It's structuring a conversation to increase the likelihood that the person who's on the other side gives you money."
