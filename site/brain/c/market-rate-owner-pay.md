# Market Rate Owner Pay

Topic: People & Operations | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's point that a founder who does a role inside the business should be paid the market rate for it, so the margins are not artificially high and the role can be handed to a hire.

## His words

> "to pay what would be market rate for the role that they're fulfilling within the business"
> [Building a $2,300,000/yr Business for a Stranger in 57 Mins @ 18:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Koq14DPXC4&t=1105s)

> "The big issue that I had was that I never asked for basically what I should have probably been paid"
> [Building a $2,300,000/yr Business for a Stranger in 57 Mins @ 17:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Koq14DPXC4&t=1044s)

> "It's super important for ANI or anybody who has a model that they intend to scale to pay what would be market rate for the role that they're fulfilling within the business."
> [Ep.906: Bootstrapped to Millions Without Loans or Investors @ 18:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIpjmvgDEWU&t=1090s)
