# Three Core Distortions

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex, drawing on Albert Ellis, says three irrational beliefs distort reality: I must get what I want when I want it, others must treat me fairly, and I must do well or I am no good.
