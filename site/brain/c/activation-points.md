# Activation Points

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: metric | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The early action that most predicts a customer staying, found by asking the longest-staying customers what gave them the most value; for Gym Launch it was a 2,000 dollar sale in the first seven days.
