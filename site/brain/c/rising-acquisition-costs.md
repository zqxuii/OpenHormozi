# Rising Acquisition Costs

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Acquisition costs rise as a business moves into colder audiences and more competitors appear, and new hires take time to get good, so the LTV to CAC ratio needs padding as the business scales.

## His words

> "Cost of getting customer, believe it or not, always goes up over time."
