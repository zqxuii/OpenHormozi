# Push and Pull Motivation

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: framework | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Push motivation is duty or obligation, while pull motivation is a pull toward something you want to serve more than yourself.

## His words

> "There's two types of motivation, if you don't think of it that way. There's push motivation, which is, let's say, duty,"
> [How To Find Your Purpose - Alex Hormozi x Tony Robbins @ 3:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5IOP8kKUpHg&t=186s)

> "where there's something out there that you want to serve more than yourself, that'll get you up early, keep you up late"
> [How To Find Your Purpose - Alex Hormozi x Tony Robbins @ 3:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5IOP8kKUpHg&t=218s)

> "But willpower only goes so far. That's push."
> [Tony Robbins x Alex Hormozi: Self Actualized Selling Masterc @ 30:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zPmNYLb1kJ4&t=1834s)
