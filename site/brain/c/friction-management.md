# Friction Management

Topic: People & Operations | Type: tactic | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to make the things you do not want inconvenient and the things you want convenient, so you do more of the right ones.

## His words

> "make it inconvenient to do all the things that you don't want to do and make it more convenient to do the things that you do"
> [It's Actually Pretty Easy to Get Ahead of 99% of People | Al @ 2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tigt75AcLLA&t=156s)

> "Add friction to whatever sabotages you, take away friction from whatever makes your life better"
> [Give Me 42 Minutes To Destroy Your Self-Doubt @ 34:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U4qrZYiYdc8&t=2048s)

> "you want to put lots of friction in the way of doing the things that you don't want"
> [What really matters at the end of life (life & death discuss @ 36:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uj5XKwW0Vbs&t=2176s)
