# Discretionary Effort

Topic: Wealth & Investing | Type: principle | Seen in 6 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says if people work only for the paycheck you miss most of their effort, since people have far more discretionary effort than the amount needed to keep their job, and freedom and investment unlock it.

## His words

> "if the only reason someone does something is for the paycheck you're going to miss out on the vast majority of human effort which is discretionary effort"
