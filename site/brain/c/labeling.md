# Labeling

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Labeling means naming what a prospect says, such as an objection, and reframing it early so it does not detonate at the close; proactive labels are positive traits you attribute and the prospect agrees to.
