# Delayed Gratification

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: mindset | Seen in 18 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex calls the ability to delay a moment of reward the meta skill that overshadows the rest, and says the first feedback loop is what keeps people going.
