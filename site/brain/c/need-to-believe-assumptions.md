# Need To Believe Assumptions

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to build a plan on the fewest need-to-believe assumptions, because each new assumption lowers the chance the plan works.

## His words

> "make a plan on like the fewest need-to-believes as possible"
