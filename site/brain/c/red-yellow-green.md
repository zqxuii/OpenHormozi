# Red Yellow Green

Topic: People & Operations | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Sort each task into green (someone can take it now), yellow (needs a one-time project or process first) or red (you lack the skill or the person), and clear green and yellow first.
