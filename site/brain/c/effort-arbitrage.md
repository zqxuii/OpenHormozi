# Effort Arbitrage

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Maintaining a skill takes far less effort than growing it, so Hormozi says spreading effort thinly across many skills makes no progress; the saved effort should go to one priority.

## His words

> "if you do a four out of 10 on 10 things you will make progress on none"
> [How Small Wins Compound Into Greatness - Alex Hormozi (4K) @ 134:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WO5m-roVzjg&t=8052s)

> "the amount of effort that it takes to maintain a skill versus the amount of effort it takes to grow a skill is like on10th the amount of effort"
> [The Regret You Don’t See Coming In Your 20's & 30's - Alex H @ 10:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DEd7MjYkIc0&t=601s)
