# Solution Delivery Vehicle

Topic: Offers | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Brainstorming, for each solution to a prospect problem, a specific way to include it in the product, without yet worrying about feasibility.

## His words

> "once you want to do that you want to create solution delivery vehicle so for every solution that you just came up with think about specifically how could you provide that solution"
