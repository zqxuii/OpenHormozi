# Brand Bouquet

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

A brand is like a bouquet of flowers: separate elements such as products, values, and people only become a brand once you repeatedly pair them, and removing the pairings makes the brand disappear.
