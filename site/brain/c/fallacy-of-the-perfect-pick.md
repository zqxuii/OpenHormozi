# Fallacy of the Perfect Pick

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 7 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says people wait for the one right choice, but progress comes from the habit of acting and improving, and waiting to begin never gets anyone anywhere.
