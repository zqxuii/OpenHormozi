# Motivation From Deprivation

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's view that motivation comes from lacking something, so wanting a million dollars means you do not yet have one, and surrounding yourself with people who earn more is a way to create that drive.
