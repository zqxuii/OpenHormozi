# Loss Leader

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Sell a well-known product below the price the market expects, accepting a loss, so the customer comes in and buys higher-margin extras.

## His words

> "a loss leader is basically where you sell a very well understood product and you sell it for less than the market understands the prices for so you sell it at a loss"
> [How to get rich this Black Friday @ 0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHrAjWni65E&t=32s)

> "they sold them cheaper than everyone else did but they made all the profit up on the other ancillary upsells"
> [How to get rich this Black Friday @ 2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mHrAjWni65E&t=154s)
