# Many-Sided Die

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: story | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's analogy: each player has a die with many red sides and some green sides, and every die turns green if you roll it enough times, so the die you were given matters less than your number of rolls.
