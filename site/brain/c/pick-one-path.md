# Pick One Path

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says a founder should commit to one path because pursuing several at once guarantees failure at all of them, and strategy is choosing where to allocate limited resources.

## His words

> "pursuing all of them at the same time will virtually guarantee that you don't succeed at any of them"
> [I Helped Business Owners Overcome Their Fear of Failure @ 13:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0_Gf5v8DEMY&t=791s)

> "trying to do both will be very hard because what'll happen is you'll start to do some online stuff"
