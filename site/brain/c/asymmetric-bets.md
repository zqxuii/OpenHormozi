# Asymmetric Bets

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: principle | Seen in 13 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi's betting logic is to seek bets with no downside and limited upside and take as many as possible, so that losses are small and wins compound.

## His words

> "the perfect way of betting is having no downside and having a limited upside"

> "if you have uh a 10% chance at 100 to one return, you should take that bet every time"
