# Warm Outreach

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 33 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Message people you know with a specific compliment and your niche sentence, then ask who they know that could benefit; Alex says doing this for up to four hours a day yields five customers.
