# Resilience

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: metric | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Resilience is how long it takes you to return to normal after your behaviour has changed because of something bad.

## His words

> "So once bad happens to you and you start acting out, how long does it take you to stop acting out? That's what resilience is. It's a measure of time."
