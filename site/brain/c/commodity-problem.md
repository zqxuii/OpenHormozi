# Commodity Problem

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Selling a commodity forces price competition because clients swap to the cheapest provider, so the presenter says to avoid being commoditized and build a differentiated product.

## His words

> "the problem is that when selling commodities you are forced to sell them cheaper to stay competitive"
