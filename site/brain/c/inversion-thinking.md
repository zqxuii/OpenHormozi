# Inversion Thinking

Topic: Offers | Type: tactic | Seen in 9 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi's process of listing how you would guarantee losing and then flipping each item into what you would do to win, which he attributes to Einstein and Charlie Munger.
