# Training Arbitrage

Topic: People & Operations | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's view that a service business makes money by training low-skill people quickly, then paying them less than the market rate for the skill they now have.

## His words

> "the arbitrage of the business is being able to get someone in who has low skill, train them up on your system"
> [Building a $2,300,000/yr Business for a Stranger in 57 Mins @ 45:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Koq14DPXC4&t=2710s)

> "And the arbitrage of the business is being able to get someone in who has low skill, train them up on your system and then be able to charge a higher rate for what that skill commands in the marketplace."
> [Ep.906: Bootstrapped to Millions Without Loans or Investors @ 45:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rIpjmvgDEWU&t=2730s)
