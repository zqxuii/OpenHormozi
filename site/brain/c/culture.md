# Culture

Topic: People & Operations | Type: principle | Seen in 70 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex defines culture operationally as the rules that reinforce behaviour, what you reward and punish, and says a mediocre strategy with great culture beats a great strategy with terrible culture.
