# Decision Speed

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 8 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says setting decisions to end of day rather than end of week lets you move much faster through life.

## His words

> "times the rate of other people by simply changing when you say you're going to make a decision"
