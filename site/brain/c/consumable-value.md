# Consumable Value

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Ads were treated as consumable value: the speaker spent about 50k a month testing ads in ten markets, then licensed the winners, so the cost after the test was close to zero.
