# Three Beliefs Before Buying

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says a prospect must really want the goal and believe three things: that the product will get them the goal their way, that others will support them, and that it will work for them, with proof making disbelief unreasonable.
