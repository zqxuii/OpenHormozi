# Nail It Before You Scale It

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to fix churn and operations before adding volume or locations, and to open a new location only after the old one grows without him for six months.

## His words

> "Nail it before you scale it. So, this goes to the whole rush thing"
> [How to Start a Business From Nothing (Thank Me Later) @ 42:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=unshZobTt6Q&t=2532s)

> "don't go to a new one until the old one is growing without you for 6 months straight"
> [How to Start a Business From Nothing (Thank Me Later) @ 42:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=unshZobTt6Q&t=2562s)

> "if you're losing customers and churning them out, why would you scale it"
