# Classic Upsell

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The classic upsell offers the solution to the customer's next problem the moment they become aware of it, using the you can't have X without Y structure; current customers buy more readily than strangers.
