# Give to Ask Ratio

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 6 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says the audience should see about 3.5 pieces of giving for every ask, and that starting with that ratio and adding patience compounds over time.
