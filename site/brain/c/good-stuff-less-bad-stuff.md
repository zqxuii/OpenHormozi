# Good Stuff Less Bad Stuff

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Describe the good outcome the customer wants (the upside) and the bad things they want to avoid, since people act to get good things or to avoid bad ones, with specific pain making the copy believable.

## His words

> "you either have to take away the bad stuff that they don't want or give them more good"
