# Customer Onboarding

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

A choreographed first days-to-month experience that sets expectations, reconfirms the buying decision and walks the customer through what they bought, because many cancel in the first 24 hours.
