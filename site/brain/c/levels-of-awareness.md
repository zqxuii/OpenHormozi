# Levels of Awareness

Topic: Offers | Type: framework | Seen in 6 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Customers sit at levels of awareness from completely unaware, through problem aware and solution aware, to product aware and most aware, and copy should speak to the level the reader is at.
