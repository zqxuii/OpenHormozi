# Talent Arbitrage

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says paying top talent a premium beats hiring many cheap people, so talent is the biggest arbitrage; big companies compete for talent, and talent works best with a mission people care about.
