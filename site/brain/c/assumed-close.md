# Assumed Close

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Asking a question that assumes the customer does not want more, such as 'you don't want anything else, do you?', so that the customer says no and thereby agrees to the add-on. Alex says it can produce 90 percent plus take rates.
