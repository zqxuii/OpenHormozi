# Call to Action

Topic: Sales | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says a good call to action tells the audience what to do and gives a reason to do it now, and any reason works better than none.

## His words

> "Good CTAs have two things. One, what to do, and two, reasons to do it right now"
> [$100M Leads Full Audiobook | Alex Hormozi @ 58:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o0bFrtrOnZU&t=3495s)

> "any reason, even bad ones, tends to work better than no reason at all"
