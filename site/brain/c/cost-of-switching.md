# Cost of Switching

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Switching to a new opportunity means a new business starts behind the business you left, because growth gets easier when you are bigger, so sticking with one thing gives a head start.
