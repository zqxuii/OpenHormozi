# Buying Back Time

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex argues owners should pay to remove chores such as cleaning, cooking and laundry, because these cost about 96 hours a month for roughly $1,500, and that is worth it when your own time earns more than $15 an hour.
