# Ownership Compensation

Topic: People & Operations | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says to bring in exceptional department leaders and pay them from their own division's profit and loss, so they act like owners, as Amazon does with its CEOs.

## His words

> "they get compensated off the p&l of that micro business"
