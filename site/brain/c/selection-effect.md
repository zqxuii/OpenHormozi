# Selection Effect

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: principle | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says the harder a path is, the more it filters out competitors, so reframing difficulty as a selection effect makes hard work more motivating.

## His words

> "I get happier about the harder it is because I know that no one else will follow."
> [The Most Valuable 40 Minutes You’ll Spend This Year (Hormozi @ 26:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wy7CTJcfiM4&t=1605s)

> "Everything worth doing is hard. And the more worth doing it is, the harder it is."
> [The Most Valuable 40 Minutes You’ll Spend This Year (Hormozi @ 26:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wy7CTJcfiM4&t=1573s)
