# Time Arbitrage

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says outsourcing pays off when your hourly earnings are higher than what the outsourced task costs per hour, because the spare time is worth more.
