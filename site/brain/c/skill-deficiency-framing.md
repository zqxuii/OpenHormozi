# Skill Deficiency Framing

Topic: Sales | Type: mindset | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to reframe I don't know how to market as a skill gap you can close, rather than blaming the market or saying the business will not work.

## His words

> "this business won't work because the market doesn't like my stuff you say I don't know how to Market my stuff"
> [Business Was Hard Until I Understood These 4 Concepts @ 33:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F84olnKkseM&t=2006s)

> "I want you to say I don't know how to scale this and then that becomes a problem that you can solve"
> [Your Business Is NOT What You Think It Is @ 37:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rj7rzOdj84A&t=2252s)

> "I try and frame the thing that I don't know how to the problem as something I don't know how to do"
