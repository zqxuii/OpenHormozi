# Keyman Risk

Topic: People & Operations | Type: principle | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The risk that one person who is vital to operations leaves or is lost, which materially reduces the business's income; Alex says it turns an asset into a high-paying job and is a red flag to buyers.

## His words

> "a single person that is vital to the operations of the business and if they were gone the business would decrease materially"
