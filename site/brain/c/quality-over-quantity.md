# Quality Over Quantity

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says one exceptional thing beats many mediocre ones, that customers pay for value per second, and that many small golden improvements, not one silver bullet, create the outsize returns.
