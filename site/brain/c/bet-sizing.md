# Bet Sizing

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Size each business bet to the outcome you want, since a trillion-dollar goal requires going all in while smaller goals can be reached with medium bets, and never betting at all guarantees no result.
