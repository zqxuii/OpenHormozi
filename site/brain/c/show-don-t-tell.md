# Show Don't Tell

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to demonstrate the result live, such as playing a real lead call or doing the product on the prospect's own file, because people discount a promise by how unlikely it feels to them.
