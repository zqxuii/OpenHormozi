# Motivation Is Deprivation

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi's model is that people are most motivated by what they lack relative to a reference point, so predicting behaviour means looking at what someone is deprived of.

## His words

> "motivation is the equal opposite of deprivation so we are we are most motivated when we are deprived of something"
