# Leaky Bucket

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: framework | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says that once you reach about 100,000 a month in revenue, you should fix the holes in your product, retention and referrals before scaling advertising, because a leaky bucket becomes a problem later, and fixing it early sets you up for a bigger business.
