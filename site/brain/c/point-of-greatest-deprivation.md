# Point of Greatest Deprivation

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's rule that you should sell at the point of greatest deprivation, when the buyer feels the most need, rather than the point of greatest value, because the right offer at the wrong time is still the wrong offer.
