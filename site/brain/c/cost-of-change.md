# Cost of Change

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's observation is that a change to a working business typically drops output by about 20 percent before it recovers, so a change is only worth making if the potential gain is far larger.
