# Bridge Of Bricks

Topic: Offers | Type: story | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex uses a bridge metaphor where each skill is a brick, and people fail by starting new bridges before finishing one, so the key is finding the missing brick.

## His words
