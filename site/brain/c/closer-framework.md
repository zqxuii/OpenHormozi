# CLOSER Framework

Topic: Sales | Type: framework | Seen in 30 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex uses the CLOSER framework on sales calls: clarify why they are there, label the problem, overview past attempts while increasing deprivation through a pain cycle, sell the outcome, explain away objections, and reinforce the decision in the first 24 hours.
