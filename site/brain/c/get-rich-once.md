# Get Rich Once

Topic: Wealth & Investing | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The aim is to get rich only once and then keep wealth by taking small risks with large sums, since fortunes are made with big risks on small sums and preserved with small risks on big sums.
