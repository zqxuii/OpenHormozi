# Expectation Communication

Topic: People & Operations | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Tell people what you expect, repeat it, and put it in writing, because the more often you ask for something, the more important they take it to be.

## His words

> "Number one is you have to communicate them. Number two is you have to repeat the communication"
> [The SIMPLE Managerial Framework that changed my business @ 2:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IMowPVgcWbA&t=151s)

> "The faster the communication cadence that you have around the item or task or activity or metric, the more important they will deem it"
