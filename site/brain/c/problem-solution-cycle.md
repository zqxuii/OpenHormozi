# Problem Solution Cycle

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi's view: every solved problem creates the next one, so the business should be ready with the next offer at the moment the customer needs it.

## His words

> "actually solve a problem with whatever you do you also create another problem"
> [Alex Hormozi’s Blueprint To Making Money Blew My Mind @ 43:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gT2bk52F9bg&t=2592s)
