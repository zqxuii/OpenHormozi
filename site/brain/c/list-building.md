# List Building

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Building a contact list from paid software, from brokers, or by hand from places such as Yelp, Google Maps, Facebook groups and Reddit. Alex calls the hand-built list the most valuable.
