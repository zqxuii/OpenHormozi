# Lonely Chapter

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 7 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex describes a period in which you no longer fit with your old friends but do not yet have the achievements to fit with a new group, and says that this is where the most work gets done.
