# Dunning-Kruger Effect

Topic: Offers | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Leila describes the Dunning Kruger effect as people overestimating their ability before they start, which then pushes them to stick with the work until they get good.

## His words

> "people overestimate their ability to do something if you didn't overestimate it you wouldn't start doing it in the first place"
