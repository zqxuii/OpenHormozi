# Front to Back CAC Optimization

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to put most of the effort into the front end of an ad, the headline and first five seconds, because small front-end gains multiply throughput.
