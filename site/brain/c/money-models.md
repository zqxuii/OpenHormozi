# Money Models

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: framework | Seen in 90 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex calls his model client finance acquisition: one customer's gross profit pays for acquiring and delivering to that customer, so the next customer can be funded.

## His words

> "fundamentally, I call it client finance acquisition, but basically is that one customer comes embedded within it enough gross profit to pay for that customer"
