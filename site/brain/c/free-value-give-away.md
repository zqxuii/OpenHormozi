# Free Value Give-Away

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The speaker says B2B companies should give away real value such as training, tools and calculators, accepting that most people will never buy, because the free value builds the brand.
