# Brand as Association

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: belief | Seen in 9 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says a brand is not a thing but an association people make between what they know and the company they do not yet know, and that you deliberately cultivate those associations.

## His words

> "big picture brand isn't actually a thing it's an association we make between things that we know and things that people don't know which is usually our company"
