# Net Negative Churn

Topic: Money & Unit Economics | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex describes net negative churn as a business where revenue still rises each month even with no new customers, because the remaining customers spend more.

## His words

> "every single month if you acquired no new customers youwould still make more money"
