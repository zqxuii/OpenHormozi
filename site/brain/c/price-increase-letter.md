# Price Increase Letter

Topic: Offers | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to announce price rises with a letter that lists what customers get, grandfathers existing customers for a period, and to measure conversion on a large enough sample before deciding, rather than reacting to the first few no's.
