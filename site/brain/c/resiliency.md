# Resiliency

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

The speaker defines resiliency as how long it takes to return to baseline behaviour after a bad event, separate from toughness, which is how long your fuse is.

## His words

> "I define resiliency as the amount of time after an aversive stimulus, after a bad thing happens, that you return back to baseline behavior"
