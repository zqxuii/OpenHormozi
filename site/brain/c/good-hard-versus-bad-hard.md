# Good Hard Versus Bad Hard

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says good hard is iterating toward assumptions you hold from first principles, while bad hard is when those underlying assumptions prove incorrect and you must pivot.
