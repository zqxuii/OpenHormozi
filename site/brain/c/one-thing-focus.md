# One Thing Focus

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: principle | Seen in 6 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's rule that a business should identify the one most important thing and communicate and deliver it as well as possible, and that a CEO who cannot name it has failed.
