# Menu Upsell

Topic: Sales | Type: tactic | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex calls the menu upsell his favourite sales process for one-conversation sales, where the seller says what the customer does not need and prescribes what they do need, offering a choice between options. Caleb adds that it builds trust because not every product is for every person.
