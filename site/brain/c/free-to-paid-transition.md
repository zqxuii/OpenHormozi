# Free to Paid Transition

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Start by working for free to get reps, then stop taking free work once demand exceeds supply and begin charging.

## His words

> "Suck at something. Work for free lots of times. Suck less. Wait until people ask for free work and you can't take them on."
