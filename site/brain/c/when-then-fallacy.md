# When Then Fallacy

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: belief | Seen in 5 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says waiting until you are less busy to start assumes you will be less busy later, so start when you are busiest so the habit survives.

## His words

> "you can use something that I like to call the when then fallacy"
