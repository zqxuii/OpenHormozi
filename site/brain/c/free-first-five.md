# Free First Five

Topic: Offers | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi recommends giving the first five customers of any new product or service for free, in exchange for use, feedback and a review, to build reps and reputation.

## His words

> "whenever you launch a new product or service, make the first five free"

> "People are far more forgiving when you haven't charged anything"
