# Prospect Over Sale

Topic: Sales | Type: mindset | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says to keep the prospect and their goals, not the sale, as the priority, and to be happy when a prospect decides for themselves, even if that is not with you.

## His words

> "they don't care about you they only care about them and they will talk about themselves as long as they'll let them"

> "helping someone make a decision to help themselves does not mean buying from me"
> [The Best SALES TRAINING On The Internet @ 20:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NcD2t9qt-fM&t=1229s)
