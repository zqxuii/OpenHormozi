# Enterprise Value Drivers

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex ties every goal to one of three things: more customers, more money per customer, or lower risk of losing either over the long term, which raises enterprise value.
