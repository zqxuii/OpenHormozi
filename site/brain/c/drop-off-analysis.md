# Drop-off Analysis

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Before changing anything, look at the webinar's attendance, drop-off points and engagement spikes to find where people leave, then fix the biggest drop-off first.
