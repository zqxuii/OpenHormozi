# Asymmetric Payoff

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: principle | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi argues that bets with a small chance of a very large payoff are worth taking, and that people wrongly judge risk by counting failures rather than the absolute return.

## His words

> "Given a 10% chance of a 100 times payoff, you should take that bet every time"
