# Decoy Offer

Topic: Offers | Type: tactic | Seen in 8 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi describes a decoy as a dummy offer that makes the cheaper offer look less attractive and steers buyers to the expensive one.

## His words

> "It's a dummy offer that gets customers to ignore your cheap stuff because they like your expensive stuff even more"
> [If You Missed it... I'm Launching Live. Again @ 64:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B2QbLzHvKbg&t=3883s)

> "Put your decoy offer and premium offer side by side so people can see how much more valuable your premium is"
> [How to SELL So Good, Saying No Feels Stupid [$100M Money - A @ 4:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SKbND1wH80k&t=247s)
