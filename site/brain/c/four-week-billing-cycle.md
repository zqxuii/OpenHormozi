# Four-Week Billing Cycle

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: tactic | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex has gyms switch from monthly billing to every four weeks, which gives 13 billing cycles a year instead of 12 and adds about 8 percent a year.

> "we get all of our independent operators to switch from you know monthly billing to every four weeks or every two week billing"
