# Sadness Versus Anxiety

Topic: Mindset & Self | Type: framework | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Hormozi says sadness is a perceived lack of options and anxiety is many options with no priorities; the cure for sadness is deciding the next action.
