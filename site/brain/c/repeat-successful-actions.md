# Repeat Successful Actions

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: principle | Seen in 4 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says newer entrepreneurs fail to repeat successful actions, whether they found them themselves or were taught them, and that reminders matter more than new teaching.
