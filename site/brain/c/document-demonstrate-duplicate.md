# Document Demonstrate Duplicate

Topic: People & Operations | Type: tactic | Seen in 7 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's training method: write the job as a checklist, demonstrate it while the hire watches, then have them duplicate it while you watch, adjusting the checklist as you go.

## His words

> "first you have to document everything that you do to successfully do the job and that's step-by step into a checklist then you demonstrate"
> [The Man That Makes Millionaires: Turn $0 to $10k With This S @ 58:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kl-I7sUcAOY&t=3495s)

> "you have the person who's teaching you documents the skill that's step one"
> [How to Get Ahead of 99% of People @ 39:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nh8Oc7ERdIU&t=2397s)

> "step two is that they do it in front of you so they demonstrate it and the third piece is duplicate"
> [How to Get Ahead of 99% of People @ 39:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nh8Oc7ERdIU&t=2397s)
