# Reframing Stress As Care

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: mindset | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says stress is a fact of life in growth, stagnation and decline alike, and that treating it as a sign something is wrong with you or your business is the bigger source of stress.
