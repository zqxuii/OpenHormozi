# Problems Versus Missed Opportunities

Topic: Scaling & Strategy | Type: framework | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex says problems in a business are finite and should be solved first, while missed opportunities are infinite and distract; he asks whether a thing is a missed opportunity or a problem, and says nine times in ten it is an opportunity.

## His words

> "problems in your business are actually finite you can list all the number of problems in your business by and large missed opportunities are infinite"
