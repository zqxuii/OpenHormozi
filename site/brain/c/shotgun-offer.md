# Shotgun Offer

Topic: Offers | Type: tactic | Seen in 2 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Alex's suggested buy-sell mechanism for a partnership split: one partner names a price and terms to buy the other out, and the other partner can accept the reverse purchase.
