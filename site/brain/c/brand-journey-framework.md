# Brand Journey Framework

Topic: Leads & Marketing | Type: framework | Seen in 3 videos

## The idea (paraphrase)

Caleb's framework is four questions asked backwards from the desired outcome to today: the outcome, what you must be known for, what you must do, and what you must learn.
